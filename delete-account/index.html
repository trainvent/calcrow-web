Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Delete a Calcrow account using email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Confirm your account

Sign in using the method you use for Calcrow. Signing in does not delete anything. Check the account shown before confirming permanent deletion.

Or sign in with your Calcrow email and password:

On iPhone, you can also delete your account directly in Calcrow under Settings → Delete account, including accounts using Apple sign-in.

Account to permanently delete:

Your Calcrow profile and saved settings will be deleted. Your documents and app-store subscriptions will not be deleted or cancelled.

If you cannot sign in, contact support@trainvent.com. Never send passwords, access tokens, or authorization codes.

What will be deleted

  • Your Calcrow account profile and authentication record.
  • Stored app settings associated with that account.
  • Linked cloud metadata saved specifically for your Calcrow account.

What is not automatically deleted

  • Spreadsheet files already stored on your device, in your browser downloads, or in your personal cloud storage.
  • Purchase, billing, or subscription records maintained by the app store, payment processor, or tax and accounting systems.
  • Backup copies retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance where required.

Timing

Account deletion is intended to start immediately after confirmation. Some connected systems or retained records may take additional time to disappear from backups or provider dashboards.

Need more context?

Review the Calcrow Privacy Policy for more details about how account and file data is handled.
